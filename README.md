# 用回收替换内存清理

## ① 环境

本 mod 不依赖任何基础 mod。

本 mod 支持 public beta 110 版本与 default 107 版本。

## ② 15 种语言支持

亚洲：简体中文 / 繁体中文 / 日语 / 韩语 / 泰语 / 土耳其语

欧洲与美洲：英语 / 葡萄牙语（巴西）/ 西班牙语（拉美）/ 西班牙语 / 法语 / 俄语 / 德语 / 意大利语 / 波兰语

文本标签按首字母顺序，依次为 deu、eng、esp、fra、ita、jpn、kor、pol、ptb、rus、spa、tha、tur、zhs、zht。

文本均来自《杀戮尖塔 1》，仅作简单格式调整，可能有少量错误；文本中“能量”改用塔2的写法。

## ③ 效果

将游戏中的“内存清理”卡牌的效果、卡名、卡面图片、卡面文本更换为与《杀戮尖塔 1》中卡牌“回收”相同。

本 mod 采用直接在“内存清理”原卡牌上进行修改的方法生效，并非生成新卡牌 + 禁用旧卡牌。

本 mod 影响存档：在 mod 开启时，所有历史记录中的“内存清理”卡牌都会被更换为“回收”卡牌；关闭 mod 后会恢复原状。

## ④ 影响（基于本人游戏理解）

在原版游戏所有难度下，大多数对局中，对于故障机器人职业，回收略强于内存清理；

在卡组较大、能力牌较多的对局中，内存清理可能更强（但此时回收和内存清理都是较弱的）；

在卡组较小、洗牌速度较快的对局中，回收显著强于内存清理。

对于铁甲战士，大多数对局中，回收显著强于内存清理；

对于其它职业，大多数对局中，回收略强于内存清理。

## ⑤ 兼容性与 Bug

本 mod 采用最小依赖的方式写成，正常来说与大多数 mod 兼容，且不易随着版本变化而失效。

但本 mod 不保证与盗版、修改版、移动版、老旧版本或者冷门 mod、次级 mod 的兼容性。

# Replace-Scavenge-with-Recycle

## 1\. Environment

This mod does not depend on any base mod.

This mod supports the Public Beta 110 version and the Default 107 version.

## 2\. 15 Languages Supported

Asia: Simplified Chinese / Traditional Chinese / Japanese / Korean / Thai / Turkish

Europe \& the Americas: English / Portuguese (Brazil) / Spanish (Latin America) / Spanish / French / Russian / German / Italian / Polish

The text tags, in alphabetical order, are: deu, eng, esp, fra, ita, jpn, kor, pol, ptb, rus, spa, tha, tur, zhs, zht.

All texts are taken from Slay the Spire 1, with only simple formatting adjustments; there may be minor errors. The "Energy" display uses the energy icon notation from Slay the Spire 2.

## 3\. Effects

Replaces the effect, card name, card art, and card text of the game's "Scavenge" card with those of the "Recycle" card from Slay the Spire 1.

This mod works by modifying the original "Scavenge" card directly, rather than creating a new card and disabling the old one.

This mod affects save files: while the mod is enabled, all "Scavenge" cards in historical records will be replaced with "Recycle" cards; disabling the mod restores the original state.

## 4\. Impact (Based on My Understanding of the Game)

At all ascension levels in the vanilla game, in most runs, for the Defect, Recycle is slightly stronger than Scavenge.

In runs with larger decks and more Power cards, Scavenge may be stronger (though both are relatively weak in that situation).

In runs with smaller decks and faster shuffle speeds, Recycle is significantly stronger than Scavenge.

For the Ironclad, in most runs, Recycle is significantly stronger than Scavenge.

For other characters, in most runs, Recycle is slightly stronger than Scavenge.

## 5\. Compatibility \& Bugs

This mod is written with minimal dependencies, so under normal circumstances it is compatible with most mods and unlikely to break as the game version changes.

However, compatibility is not guaranteed with pirated copies, modified versions, mobile versions, outdated versions, niche mods, or secondary mods.
